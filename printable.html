Fill the tray of ten

Friends of ten · number bonds · make 10 · for children in their first year of school

How to play (two minutes)

  1. Cut out the cupcakes and the order cards from page 2.
  2. Pick an order card. Put that many cupcakes on the tray, filling the top row first.
  3. Ask: "How many more make ten?" Let your child add cupcakes and count on: "seven, eight, nine, ten!"
  4. When that feels easy, use the oven door: cover the tray and ask the same question. Peek if they need to.
Say it like this "The tray has 6. How many more make 10?" · "Show me with your fingers." · "How did you know?" · "Let's check: count on from 6."
Keep it short. Stop while it is still fun.

What it practises: part-part-whole to 10, counting on, and recalling the pairs that make 10. Australian Curriculum AC9MFN04 (Foundation) · England Year 1: number bonds within 20 · USA K.OA.A.4 (Kindergarten). This sheet describes practice; it makes no claim about learning outcomes.
