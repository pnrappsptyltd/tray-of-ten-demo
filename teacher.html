Friends of ten in five minutes

A prototype of a small bakery game for children in their first year of school. No login, no ads, nothing leaves the device.

Play the demo

What it practises

  • Filling a tray of ten: how many more make 10 (friends of ten / number bonds / make 10).
  • Counting on from a known number.
  • Moving from counting what you can see to knowing the pair: the tray goes into the oven and the child says how many more.

Curriculum

Australia · AC9MFN04 (Foundation)Partition and combine collections up to 10 using part-part-whole relationships and subitising.
USA · K.OA.A.4 (Kindergarten)For any number from 1 to 9, find the number that makes 10 when added to the given number.
England · Year 1Represent and use number bonds and related subtraction facts within 20.
